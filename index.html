COGNOROGUE

A Cognitive Assessment Roguelike

WASD / Arrow Keys to Move • Space to Attack

Shift to Dodge • F to Block/Parry

HOW TO PLAY

Controls

A/D or Left/Right: Move

W or Up: Jump

Space: Attack

Shift: Dodge Roll (i-frames)

F: Block / Parry (timing-based)

Combat

Enemies telegraph attacks with visual cues. Learn their patterns!

Parry: Block at the right moment to stun enemies and deal bonus damage.

Dodge: Roll through attacks with invincibility frames.

Combo: Chain attacks for bonus damage.

Cognitive Assessment

Your gameplay is analyzed to measure:

  • Reaction Time
  • Pattern Recognition
  • Working Memory
  • Multitasking Ability
  • Decision Making
  • Sustained Attention
